### tl;dr

Mermaid charts, Slack messages, and PNG renderings are not stored on our servers. The only information stored in a database is information about Slack's OAuth access to your workspace. Nothing is shared with third parties.

### 1. Introduction

Thank you for choosing Mermaid Preview, a App for Slack designed to generate PNG previews of Mermaid.js charts. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this policy carefully and if you do not agree with its terms, please refrain from using our App.

### 2. Data We Collect

The App receives messages from channels where it is a member so it can detect Mermaid diagrams in code blocks. Messages that do not contain a Mermaid diagram are ignored. The only data saved in a database is the App for Slack installation details provided by Slack. This data is deleted when you uninstall the App from your workspace. We do not store Slack messages or Mermaid source.

### 3. How We Use Your Data

Mermaid charts submitted through the App UI or detected in channel messages are processed only to generate PNG previews and upload them to your Slack workspace. We do not use message or diagram data for any other purpose.

### 4. Data Storage and Retention

Mermaid charts and their PNG renderings are not stored on our servers. They are generated on the fly and immediately uploaded to your Slack workspace. Even if the upload fails or is canceled, we don't persist any copy.

### 5. Data Sharing and Third Party Access

Nothing is being shared with third parties.

### 6. User's Rights

Data Deletion: Your data is deleted when you uninstall the App from your workspace. You may contact us to request deletion of your data at any time on hello@mermaid-preview.com.

### 7. Security Measures

The Application uses encryption where possible. It only receives messages in channels where it has been invited and inspects code blocks to detect valid Mermaid source. The Application does not store those messages or any data that is not necessary for its operations.

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy would ever need to be updated, it will be done publicly in the Application's repository at https://github.com/JackuB/mermaid-preview.

Use GitHub Issues on https://github.com/JackuB/mermaid-preview or email hello@mermaid-preview.com.
